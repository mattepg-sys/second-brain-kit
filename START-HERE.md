> Any other language: just write to the AI in your language.

Welcome. This folder is a guided kit for building your own **second brain**: a set of folders and plain-text files that you and an AI assistant work on together. Over time, small automations ("flows") take information from where it arrives, process it, and deliver something more useful to where you need it.

You don't need to read anything else. An AI assistant will guide you step by step, in your language.

**Before you start:**
- **Time:** about 3–4 hours (over 2–3 sittings) to design your second brain and get a first flow working; more if you want flows that run while your computer is off. A quick start (under an hour) is also possible. You can stop and continue whenever you want.
- **Talk instead of typing:** use your AI app's microphone or your computer's or phone's dictation (Windows: Win + H; Mac: press the Fn/globe key twice; phones: the microphone on the keyboard). Long spoken answers are welcome.
- **Go at your pace:** you can answer several questions in one message, skip steps, or ask to see the map at any time.

## For you (the human): how to begin

**First:** if you downloaded this kit as a zip, **extract it** (Windows: right-click → Extract All; Mac: double-click). Work from the extracted folder, not from inside the zip.

Then pick the way that matches the AI you use.

### Option A — Your AI can open folders on your computer (recommended)
Examples: the Claude desktop app with a connected folder, OpenAI Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor, and similar tools.

1. Keep this folder anywhere for now (you'll decide its final home together with the AI, including merging it into a folder or notes vault you already use).
2. Connect or open the folder in your AI tool.
3. Write: **"Read START-HERE.md and let's begin."** (or the sentence in your language above)

### Option B — You only use a chat (ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude on the web or phone)
1. Keep the extracted folder on your computer: it will become your second brain, and you'll save files into it.
2. Start a new chat. If your assistant has "Projects" (or similar), create one and add the files there, so you don't have to upload them every time.
3. Upload two files: `START-HERE.md` and `_guide/00-for-the-ai.md`.
4. Write: **"Let's begin."** (in your language)

The AI will ask you to upload other files when it needs them, and will give you new or changed files to save. Everything works; it just takes more copying (plan roughly 60–90 minutes for a first quick flow).

### Coming back later
- **Option A:** open the folder and write **"Let's continue."**
- **Option B:** in a new chat (or your Project), upload `START-HERE.md`, `_guide/00-for-the-ai.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `_system/PROGRESS.md`. If you are building or changing a flow, add `_system/places.md` and `_system/flows.md`. Then write **"Let's continue."**

The AI will pick up exactly where you left off.

> This kit was designed and tested mainly with Claude. It is written to work with any capable AI assistant.

## For the AI assistant

You are about to guide a person through building their second brain. Before saying anything else:

1. **Read `_guide/00-for-the-ai.md` completely.** It defines your role, your principles and how to run this guide. Follow it for the whole conversation.
2. **Find out where the journey stands.**
   - File mode: read `_system/PROGRESS.md` (and `AGENTS.md`).
   - Chat mode: if the person did not upload `_system/PROGRESS.md`, treat this as a **first visit**: don't ask for it. In your first reply, ask them to upload `_guide/journey/01-welcome.md`.
   - If the journey has not started, follow `_guide/journey/01-welcome.md`. If it is in progress, greet the person briefly, summarize in two lines where you are, and continue from the "Next step" in `PROGRESS.md`.
3. **Language:** reply in the language the person wrote in. If their only message is the trigger sentence in English, open with one short line in English and the same line in two or three other likely languages, and ask which they prefer.
4. If you can open the folder but cannot see `_guide/` or `_system/` (some tools hide underscore folders), tell the person and ask them to upload the files you need (chat mode for the guide files only).

Load guide files one at a time, only when you need them. Do not read the whole `_guide/` folder in advance.
