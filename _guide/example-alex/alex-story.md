# Alex's second brain (example)

Alex is fictional, but every flow in this story is based on things real people run. Use this file to explain the map in `alex-map.html`, and to give concrete examples during the journey. Don't present Alex as a template to copy: the point is to show what is possible and how it grew.

---

## Who Alex is

- Founder of **Brightloop**, a small B2B software startup (five people).
- Part-time **master's student**; the school uses **Moodle**.
- Parent of **Leo (7)** and **Mia (4)**; shares family logistics with a partner.
- Uses: a ~$20/month AI assistant plan with folder access and scheduled tasks, Gmail and Google Calendar, a synced cloud folder, an Android phone and a laptop.

## Before

Information everywhere: email, a notes app, the Desktop, WhatsApp, paper. Alex forgot follow-ups with investors, discovered a course deadline two days late, lost a lab result PDF, and spent Sunday evenings "getting organized" instead of resting.

## The map (what you see in `alex-map.html`)

- **Comes from (10 sources):** voice notes, email (read-only), calendar, meeting recorder, the master's platform, the kids' school, the health portal, bank exports, a web clipper, a sleep tracker (paused).
- **Arrives:** `Inbox/` (everything new, including voice transcripts) and `Finance/bank-exports/` (original bank files, protected: the AI only reads them).
- **Lands:** `Brightloop/` (source of truth, deliverables, a protected contracts folder), `People/`, `Master/`, `Family/`, `Health/`, `Finance/`, `Archive/`, and planned `Newsletter/`.
- **Goes out:** "Needs you" (the backlog), draft email replies (Alex sends), a weekly study plan, the family calendar, a monthly spending summary, the investor update (checked by a second AI, sent by Alex), a reading queue on the tablet.
- **Goes further:** the morning briefing (built from the calendar, the backlog and the study plan), and planned LinkedIn posts built from the newsletter draft. Outputs can feed new flows; that's how new levels appear.

22 flows: 18 active, 3 planned, 1 paused.

## How it grew (one flow at a time)

| Month | Added | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Voice capture** → Inbox; **Inbox triage** (on demand, then daily at 05:30) | Ideas were getting lost; the Desktop was chaos |
| 2 | **Course deadlines** (Moodle calendar link) and the **weekly study plan**; **meeting capture** → Brightloop + People | A missed deadline; forgotten promises after calls |
| 3 | **Health check** (daily 05:45); **email capture** with facts and their source, and **draft replies** (06:00) | Three flows running: needed to trust them; email was eating mornings |
| 4 | **School → family calendar**; **health timeline** from portal PDFs; **morning briefing** (06:15) | Family logistics and a lost lab result |
| 5 | **Monthly spending summary** from bank CSV exports; **investor update** with a cold review; **reading queue** | Wanted clarity on money; a wrong number once reached an investor |
| Planned | Newsletter pipeline (candidates → draft → LinkedIn posts) | Waiting until the current flows are stable |
| Paused | Sleep summary | Interesting, but Alex never acted on it |

## A normal morning now

By 06:15, before Alex wakes up: the inbox is sorted (05:30), the health check has listed what needs attention (05:45), emails have been read and three drafts are ready (06:00), and a one-page briefing combines the day's calendar, deadlines, study blocks and the "needs you" list. Alex reads it with breakfast and sends two of the drafts after small edits.

## What Alex decided (owner's choices)

- Email is **read-only** for the AI; drafts are created, Alex always sends.
- Health data for the whole family **lives in the brain**; Alex accepted that the AI assistant's provider processes it, after the risk was explained.
- Bank files are **protected** (read-only); nothing connected can make payments.
- Family calendar events are created **only after Alex confirms**.
- The health check may **fix trivial things on its own** (missing READMEs, unregistered folders).

## Costs

- AI assistant plan: ~$20/month (scheduled tasks and connectors included in Alex's plan, as of October 2026).
- Dictation app: free tier at first, later ~$12–15/month.
- Everything else: free (calendar subscription, CSV exports, web clipper, a small script for the tablet).
- **About $35/month in total.** Heavier setups that call AI models many times a day through paid APIs can cost far more, so Alex checks the cost estimate of each new flow before building it.

## What Alex learned

1. **Start with capture and sorting.** Nothing else works if things don't arrive in one place.
2. **Each flow earned its place.** The sleep summary didn't, so it's paused; no guilt.
3. **The health check made the system trustworthy.** Without it, a silently broken flow went unnoticed for a week.
4. **Drafts, not sends.** It saved Alex from one embarrassing email in month 3. (Alex's choice; others automate more.)
5. **The map keeps it understandable.** With 20+ arrows, looking at one place at a time is the only way to see what's going on.
