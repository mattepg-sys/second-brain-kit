# Step 03 — Your story

**Goal:** understand the person's life, projects, information sources and pains well enough to design a second brain that fits them.
**Time:** 15–25 minutes.
**Output:** `_system/profile.md` filled in and confirmed by the person.

---

## How to interview

- **One question at a time**, each with an example answer or a default so it's easy to reply.
- **Follow their energy.** If they talk a lot about one area, go deeper there; skip what doesn't apply.
- **Accept short answers, and long ones.** You can always come back later. If they answer several questions in one message (often by voice), sort the answers yourself and ask only what's missing.
- **Names:** confirm the spelling of unusual names once (companies, people, places); dictation often mangles them. Collect them for the "Names and spellings" list in the profile.
- **Don't propose solutions yet.** If they ask "could it do X?", say "yes, likely; let's note it and look at it in the design step".
- Adapt depth to their technical comfort (from step 01).

## The questions (choose and adapt; about 8–12 in total)

**1. Life areas and projects**
"What are you juggling right now? Think of work, study, side projects, family, health, money, home… Even a rough list is fine."
Then for the 2–4 most important: "What is it, and what stage is it at?"

**2. Where information arrives**
"Where does information reach you during a normal week? For example: which email accounts, messaging apps, meetings, voice notes, documents, websites or platforms (like a university portal)."

**3. What gets lost or hurts**
"Think of the last month. What went wrong that a better system would have prevented? A forgotten follow-up, a lost document, a deadline you saw too late, too much time sorting things?"
Ask for one or two concrete examples. These are gold.

**4. Recurring work**
"What do you do again and again, every day or week, that feels mechanical?"

**5. Tools you already use**
"Which tools do you already use? AI assistants (and which plan, if you know), email and calendar provider, notes app, cloud storage (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud), any automation tools."

**6. Devices**
"Which computer and phone do you use (brand too, e.g., Xiaomi, iPhone)? And in which time zone do you live?" *(e.g., Windows laptop + Android phone, Europe/Rome)*
Also ask where they keep files today (Desktop, Documents, Google Drive, OneDrive, iCloud, Notion…): it matters for step 07.

**6b. Questions that shape the architecture** *(ask all five, briefly; they decide storage and tools, and answered late they cause rework — see `_guide/knowledge/field-notes-2026-10.md`)*
- "Should some flows work **while your computer is off** (e.g., capturing a voice note from your phone at night, a summary at 6 a.m.)?" *(default: not needed at first)*
- "Will you **dictate** a lot (voice notes, long spoken answers)?"
- "Roughly how much **free space** do you have in your cloud storage, and on your computer's disk?"
- "Do you already work in **AI chats or projects with folders connected** (e.g., one chat per project)?"
- "Are there **code projects** among the folders you might bring into the brain?"

**7. Time**
"How much time could you give to setting this up in the coming weeks, and how much per week to keep it running?" *(default: a couple of hours to start, 15–20 minutes a week)*

**8. The dream**
"If your second brain worked perfectly six months from now, what would it do for you? Describe a normal morning."

**9. Boundaries and preferences** *(optional)*
"Anything the AI should never do, or areas you'd rather keep out of it? Any preferences for tone or working hours?"
Remember: you can explain risks, but they decide.

## Reflect it back

Summarize what you heard in a short, structured way (areas and projects, sources, top pains, tools, dream), then ask:
"Did I get this right? Anything to add or correct?"

## Save and move on

1. Fill in `_system/profile.md` with the confirmed answers (free text in their document language; keep the file's structure). Copy the languages from `PROGRESS.md`. In chat mode, give it as a file to save.
2. Update `_system/PROGRESS.md`: step 03 ticked, key answers (top 3 pains, main projects), next step `04-challenge.md`.

Transition: "Thanks, I have a good picture now. Next, let's be a bit critical together: which ideas are really worth building, and which would just add work."
