# Second Brain Kit

A folder that turns any capable AI assistant into your guide for building a **second brain**: your own system of folders, plain-text files and small automations that collect information where it arrives, process it, and deliver it where it is useful.

**To start:** click **Code → Download ZIP** on GitHub, **extract** the zip (Windows: right-click → Extract All; Mac: double-click), then open `START-HERE.md` from the extracted folder. You don't need to read anything else.

---

## What you get

- **A guided conversation**, in your language, that helps you imagine what your second brain could do, challenges your ideas, and helps you decide what is really worth building.
- **A map**: you draw the second brain you want (places and the flows between them), and the AI reviews it with you and suggests tools, costs and the best first step.
- **A working base** in a folder you already use every day, with a first automation that actually saves you time.
- **A repeatable cycle** to add the next automation whenever you want, and to restructure everything as your needs change.
- **A library of ideas** (work, study, family, health, finances, content, job search, life admin), ready-made recipes (including a phone voice bot that works with the computer off), optional organizing patterns, and an example second brain, Alex's, for inspiration.
- **Field notes** from a real build, so your AI anticipates the usual problems (storage, moving folders, connector limits) instead of discovering them with you.

## Is it for you? What to expect

- **Who it's for:** anyone juggling several things (work, study, side projects, family) who wants information to stop getting lost and routine work to happen on its own. No coding needed; technical people get more options.
- **What you need:** an AI assistant (a paid plan helps for automations, but you can start free), a computer, and ideally a cloud folder (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud). Most flows cost nothing or are included in plans you already have; you'll always see the cost direction before deciding.
- **Time, honestly:**
  - about **3–4 hours**, best over 2–3 sittings, to design your second brain and get a first flow that runs when you ask;
  - about **8–10 hours** in total if your flows must run on their own while your computer is off (the extra time is infrastructure: storage, a cloud script, keys);
  - or a **quick start**: a first simple flow in under an hour, design later.
- **Tip:** answering by **voice dictation** makes the conversation much faster. You can also answer many questions in one long message, or skip what doesn't apply.

## How it works

- Everything is **plain Markdown files** in a normal folder: no lock-in, readable by any AI and any editor.
- `AGENTS.md` holds the rules every AI follows in your brain. `CLAUDE.md` and `GEMINI.md` point to it.
- `_guide/` is the guide itself. Keep it: new flows, changes and your map are built from it.
- `_system/` is your brain's machinery: your profile, the registry of places and flows, progress and logs.

## Works with

- AI tools that can open folders (e.g., Claude desktop app, OpenAI Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor): best experience.
- Chat-only assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude on web or phone): fully usable, with some copying of files.
- For technical people: always-on agents (e.g., Hermes Agent on a home server or VPS) can run the same brain 24/7; the brain is plain Markdown with `AGENTS.md` rules and one `SKILL.md` per flow, which such agents read directly.

Designed and tested mainly with Claude; written to work with any capable assistant.
